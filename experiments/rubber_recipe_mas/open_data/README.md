# Open SBR/NR tire-tread formulation data

This 20-row table is a manually digitized research dataset derived from Figures
4, 10, 11, and 14 in:

Jawad K. Oleiwi, Mohammed S. Hamza, and Nassier A. Nassir, "A Study of The
Effect of Carbon Black Powder on The Physical Properties of SBR/NR Blends Used
In Passenger Tire Treads", *Engineering & Technology Journal*, 29(5), 2011.
Zenodo record: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3838695. The record declares
CC BY 4.0.

The source studies a full 5 x 4 grid: NR content of 0, 25, 50, 75, or 100 phr
and N220 carbon black of 20, 40, 60, or 80 phr. SBR is `100 - NR`. The remaining
ingredients are fixed in every formulation: zinc oxide 5 phr, stearic acid 2,
TMQ antioxidant 1.5, 6PPD antiozonant 1.5, process oil 5, sulfur 5, TMTD 2, and
reclaim 5.

The plotted values were read to the visible graph resolution. They are not the
authors' raw instrument files and must be treated as approximate. The swelling
values correspond to the final 1006-hour values discussed in the paper. Thermal
diffusivity was not transcribed because the figure label and prose use
inconsistent units. The prototype therefore predicts only thermal conductivity,
oil swelling, water swelling, and specific gravity.

This dataset is suitable for demonstrating interpolation inside the published
NR/SBR/N220 domain. It is not sufficient to establish tread safety, durability,
strength, abrasion, rolling resistance, wet grip, or production readiness.
